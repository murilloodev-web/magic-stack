# O Amanhã Corporativo · The Corporate Tomorrow

A grounded **Cyberpunk RED** setting for the Magic Stack: the whole world in 2126, divided between corporate states (ESTEMPs) and the leftovers of national governments (REGESTs), plus the opening of a campaign at the Descoberto reservoir that supplies Brasília. Written in Portuguese (`data/`), translated into English (`data/en/`).

## What the build checks
- Every character sheet follows the Complete Package (stats 2–8 adding up to 62, skill levels 1–6, at most 86 skill points); HP, Seriously Wounded, Death Save and Humanity are computed, and Humanity uses the fixed losses of the grounded implant catalogue (`data/implants.lua`).
- The opening adventure (`data/flow.lua`): every scene is reachable and every path reaches an **open** scene (the adventure has no written ending yet).
- The board: every pixel of `data/worldgrid.lua` has an owner in `data/territories.lua`, every REGEST and landmark is on the map, every owner has an article.
- Links, citations, unused sources, missing translations, as in the other books.

## Files
```
book.lua, contrib.lua        shelf card, which forms each chapter offers, and this book's own forms
build.lua                    validator and site generator (Cyberpunk RED rules, open scenes, the board)
ui.lua                       interface text for the sheets, the board and the boxes, PT/EN
data/                        the book in Portuguese; data/en/ the English edition
data/worldgrid.lua           the world as 2° pixels (generated by tools/worldgrid.py, Natural Earth outlines)
data/territories.lua         ESTEMP colours and army counts, the 20 REGESTs, landmarks
tools/map.lua                the world board (inline SVG) and the mini maps
tools/localmap.lua           the Descoberto reservoir map
tools/banner.lua             the cover banner
tools/cover.lua              writes cover.html (run: lua tools/cover.lua > cover.html)
```
